## Why

Research teams live in two worlds: the Files-app world of project managers and
clinicians, and the git/Datalad world of data engineers. Today they bridge those
worlds by hand — export, upload, re-import, email. Nextcloud Repositories removes
the bridge entirely: the folder *is* the working tree of the repository, the
repository *is* what you clone, and both sides see one attributed history.
